# Photo and model attribution

No photo fixtures or model weights are included in this PWA scaffold. Before adding demo imagery, record for each source:

| Asset / bundle | Creator or source URL | License / permission | Real or synthetic | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pending collection | — | Must be verified before use | — | Do not commit unlicensed farmer or web images. |

The PWA captures new in-app images for a farmer-submitted claim. Voice notes are optional and are stored only; transcription is not implemented. Client-side signatures support idempotency and do not provide security attestation.
